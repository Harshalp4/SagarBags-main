HERO BACKGROUND VIDEO INSTRUCTIONS
==================================

To add a background video to the hero section:

1. RENAME your video file to: hero-background.mp4

2. PLACE the video file in this folder:
   /assets/videos/hero-background.mp4

3. VIDEO REQUIREMENTS:
   - Format: MP4 (H.264 codec recommended)
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
   - Duration: 15-30 seconds (will loop)
   - File size: Keep under 10MB for fast loading
   - Content: Lifestyle shots of people using bags

4. POSTER IMAGE:
   Also add a poster image (first frame or key frame) at:
   /assets/images/hero-poster.jpg
   - This shows while video loads
   - Used on mobile instead of video (for performance)

5. FREE STOCK VIDEO SOURCES:
   - Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/videos/
   - Pixabay: https://pixabay.com/videos/
   - Coverr: https://coverr.co/

   Search terms:
   - "business bag"
   - "office laptop bag"
   - "travel bag"
   - "shopping bag"
   - "corporate lifestyle"
   - "business professional"

6. VIDEO OPTIMIZATION TIPS:
   - Use HandBrake (free) to compress video
   - Target bitrate: 2-4 Mbps
   - Remove audio track (video is muted anyway)

7. RECOMMENDED VIDEO IDEAS:
   - Person walking with laptop bag in office
   - Professional carrying travel bag
   - Close-up of bag textures and quality
   - People shopping/traveling with bags
   - Montage of different bag styles

NOTE: Until you add a video, the hero will show the
dark gradient background which still looks good!
